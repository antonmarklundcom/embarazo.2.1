# U7 — week hero v2: background themes, fruit toggle, relative scale

**Built**
- `lib/hero/themes.ts` (pinned): six themes — `halo` (default), `nanduti`, `cielo`,
  `estevia`, `alas`, `estrellas` — as CSS gradients + inline SVG, **zero raster assets**.
  Each declares its own ink and scrim; `estrellas` is the one light-ink theme.
- `components/hero/ThemeBackdrop.tsx` draws the motifs; `ThemeSheet` is a bottom sheet with
  the six swatches and the fruit toggle; `ThemeChip` opens it from the hero card itself.
- Preferences on the Dexie `profile` row (`heroTheme`, `showComparison`), read through
  `useLiveQuery` exactly as `locale` is — synced for free, defaults on SSR, no version bump.
- `lib/hero/scale.ts` (pure, 14 tests): the larger subject fills the box, the smaller is
  drawn at its true fraction, with a legibility floor that clamps and then **captions**
  "tamaño real ≈ 0,01 cm". Week 20's crown-rump → crown-heel switch is captioned, not smoothed.
- `lib/seed/comparisons.json`: weeks 3–42, `{week, item, itemCm, imageSrc}`, 28 object files
  for 40 weeks. Registered in `validate-content`.
- `WeekHeroImage` composites an alpha render over the theme; `HeroSubject` does the same
  inside the home screen's progress ring, with `fetchPriority="high"` (it is the LCP element).
- `optimize:images` is alpha-preserving and strips a `src/` segment;
  `public/assets/semanas/README.md` is the input/output table for U10 and the founder.

**Decisions**
- **The prompt's premise about the home hero was wrong and the plumbing changed accordingly.**
  It says `app/(app)/page.tsx` "already mounts `WeekHeroImage`"; it does not — the home hero is
  an inline circular ring (`WeekHero`) and `WeekHeroImage` is only on `/semana/[n]`. Rather
  than leave home unthemed (which fails the exit criterion), the inside of the ring became one
  component, `HeroSubject`. The change to `page.tsx` is two elements and one deletion — the
  ring, the stats and the link are untouched.
- `object-contain` on the big card, `object-cover` in the ring: a contained image inside a
  circle leaves four corners of bare theme, and a covered one on the card crops the subject
  the background exists to frame.
- The comparison figure's baby is a proportional blob, not a silhouette asset. With no renders
  in the repo the SHAPE carries the whole claim — it is the right size next to the fruit — and
  it needs no file to be true.
- `comparisons.json` is exempted in `contentDebt`'s `NOT_A_COLLECTION` rather than given a debt
  row: it has no placeholder gate and no surface that can go dark, is complete for every week
  by test, and a row would read 0% forever and teach a founder to skim the list.
- `itemCm` values are my own, market-realistic for Paraguay and approximate by design; a test
  bounds them and asserts the trend rather than each figure.

**Known issues**
- `public/assets/semanas/` and `/comparaciones/` are still empty, so every `<img>` 404s and the
  fallback is what everyone sees. That is production today and the e2e asserts it looks
  deliberate; U10 and the founder fill it.
- Contrast was checked by eye across the six themes, not with a measured audit.
- The E2 share card keeps its flat card, per the prompt's backlog note.

**Verification** — `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm run lint`, `npm test` (1041), `npm run
validate:content` (15 files), `npm run build` with `DATABASE_URL` unset, full Playwright suite
(124 passed) including four new hero specs: theme repaints with no reload and survives one,
the fruit toggle hides the figure while the label stays, the theme follows home → `/semana/24`,
and the hero looks finished with no renders present.
