# U4 — Tool depth: contractions 5-1-1, kicks nudge

## Built
- `lib/tools/contractions.ts`: `assess511(entries, now, options)` — pure,
  timezone-free, tolerant of an in-progress contraction via
  `options.inProgressStartedAt`; suppressed below `MIN_WEEK_FOR_HINT` (37).
- `lib/tools/kicks.ts`: `kickBaseline(sessions)` (median kicks/10min over the
  last 7 completed sessions, `null` under 3) and `kickNudge(today, baseline)`.
- Both modules export a `BilingualText` hint constant (es + jopara `gn` draft).
- `/herramientas/contracciones`: renders the 5-1-1 hint under
  `<MedicalReviewByline />`, fed by the last 20 entries plus the running
  timer (if any) and `profile.week`.
- `/herramientas/pataditas`: renders the nudge under `<MedicalReviewByline />`,
  comparing the most recently *completed* session against the prior 6.
- `e2e/tool-depth.spec.ts`: seeds `contractionEntries`/`kickSessions` via raw
  IndexedDB (the `symptom-insight.spec.ts` pattern) and asserts both the
  present and the silent case for each tool.
- `scripts/gen-guarani-review.mts`: new section 6 ("Herramientas — 5-1-1 y
  pataditas"); `docs/GUARANI-REVIEW.md` regenerated (80 phrases).

## Decisions
- The weight-gain band in D7 stays OUT — reviewer-gated per PR-19, not built
  in this unit.
- `assess511`'s in-progress tolerance is an explicit optional param
  (`inProgressStartedAt`) rather than inferring "still running" from a
  zero-duration entry already in the array, since real entries never have one.
- The kicks nudge is computed off the most recently *completed* session, not
  the one currently in progress — a session that hasn't finished has no rate
  to compare yet, and the nudge should persist as a banner after finishing,
  not disappear once she leaves the counting screen.
- Reused `lib/emergency.ts`'s existing Guaraní phrasing for "se mueve menos de
  lo habitual" in the kicks hint, for consistency across the app's safety
  copy.

## Known issues
- Both new `gn` strings are hand-written jopara, pending native-speaker
  review like everything else on `docs/GUARANI-REVIEW.md`.
- `assess511`'s duration median ignores an in-progress contraction's duration
  (unknown by definition); if she is mid-contraction when the pattern first
  qualifies on gap alone, the hint may lag by one contraction versus counting
  its live elapsed time.
