# Founder decisions on the September 2026 feature ideas

Recorded 2026-09-02 from the founder's replies to eight proposed
improvements for Paraguayan users. Build sessions read this before proposing
features; it says what is approved, deferred and rejected, and why.

| # | Idea | Decision | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Public system first in the directory** — USF, hospitales regionales, maternidades MSPBS and IPS as first-class directory entries with what each offers for free | **Approved** | Needs new directory categories (`lib/directoryCategories.ts`) plus real, consented entries. Same publication gate as private listings. |
| 2 | **"Tu próximo control" guided screen** — for each scheduled control: what happens, what to bring, which labs/vaccines are due, three questions to ask; tied to the carné fields | **Approved** | Reviewer content per control. Builds on the existing appointments and carné tools. |
| 3 | **WhatsApp weekly message** | **Deferred — cost** | Per-person messages through the WhatsApp Business API are paid per message and cannot be free for all members. Free alternatives: Web Push (already built) for the personalised weekly, and a **WhatsApp Channel** (free, one-way broadcast, unlimited followers) for general weekly content and virality. Revisit paid API only as a sponsored or premium option. |
| 4 | **Read aloud** | **Approved for Spanish now** | Web Speech API (`speechSynthesis`), device voices, works offline when the voice is installed. Guaraní later: the founder is training a Guaraní voice for another app; leave a hook for a custom audio source. |
| 5 | **Seasonal arbovirus alert by department** | **Approved, data question open** | `prompts/gemini-research-dengue-data.md` researches whether MSPBS data can be fetched automatically. Until answered: ship a **season-based banner** (no data needed) plus a hand-edited `alerts.json` by department behind the usual gate. |
| 6 | **"Cuánto me va a costar" estimator** | **Deferred — no price data yet** | Build when the price guide has reviewed data. |
| 7 | **Adolescent track** | **Not decided** | Not raised by the founder; stays in the backlog. |
| 8 | **After-birth paperwork tracker + birth preparation** | **Approved, widened** | Two tools: (a) documents and steps after birth (certificado de nacido vivo, Registro Civil, cédula, IPS enrolment of the baby, PAI card); (b) **birth preparation by place of birth** — what documents and what to bring depending on hospital MSPBS, IPS, or sanatorio privado, extending guide #3 (qué llevar al sanatorio). Reviewer signs the medical parts; the documents lists need a source per line. |

Not on the table: a public forum; any AI feature touching medical questions
beyond the walled-off assistant that already exists.
