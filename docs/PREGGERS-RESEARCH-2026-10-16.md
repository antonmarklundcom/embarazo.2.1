# Preggers: official-source business research for Mi Bebé

Research date and source access: **16 October 2026**. Scope: public official website, Swedish app-store listings, company releases and the named partner's announcement. This is business research and a set of proposals, not authorization to change Mi Bebé's product, commercial policy or data practices.

The most useful pattern is a relevant weekly experience supported by practical tools and family participation. Public material does not prove that any individual feature caused growth, retention or premium conversion. Mi Bebé should improve the usefulness and discoverability of its existing functions before adding a competitor's feature inventory.

## Method and evidence limits

- **Official fact/claim** means the linked primary source currently states it. A developer's product or scale claim is not independently audited evidence.
- **Historical evidence** carries the original announcement date. Finding an old release today does not establish an active agreement today.
- **Inference/proposal** is our interpretation or an adaptation for Mi Bebé, not an observed business result.
- No account was created, app installed, subscription purchased or private onboarding inspected. No competitor imagery, proprietary text or logos were copied into the app.
- The exact current onboarding screens, field order, consent presentation, paywall timing, trial offer and premium entitlement matrix remain **unverified**. Public promotional pages do not establish those flows.

## Prioritized adaptations for Mi Bebé's current stage

The following are proposals. They build on the app's existing account setup, weekly content, practical tools and family sharing; they are not claims that those functions are missing. The approved visual/image work is documented separately in [the design brief](DESIGN-IMAGERY-2026-10-16.md).

| Priority | Practical adaptation | Small validation step; no new tracking required |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Make the first useful moment clear after the existing required account, consent and pregnancy setup: current week, readable original imagery and one useful next action. Preserve Google login and all setup requirements. | In a small owner-approved moderated session, observe whether a participant can identify her week and find her next practical action without help. Record consented observations rather than adding analytics. |
| 2 | Make one weekly return loop coherent: development, an appropriate short tip, a relevant existing guide and preparation for the next control. Use current personalized ranking and notification preferences. | Ask pilot participants what they came back to do and which card helped. Do not infer actual reminder delivery from a successful cron job or browser fixture. |
| 3 | Explain the existing family benefit through practical participation: an invitation, allowed weekly information, companion tasks and a clear way to remove access. Improve discoverability before adding more sharing. | In a consented paired-account test, ask owner and recipient what each can see and do. Preserve current server-enforced role boundaries and avoid exposing additional clinical fields. |
| 4 | Prioritize a small, locally useful and reviewed content collection over a large library. Use Spanish-for-Paraguay presentation and original imagery tied to existing text. Keep review gates intact. | Ask users to locate a guide they actually need, then identify missing topics for editorial review. In the current source, the eight guides exist; food lookup drafts remain gated by review. See [guide source](../lib/seed/articles.ts) and [food publication gate](../lib/seed/food.ts). |
| 5 | Test local distribution through the existing public guides/weekly pages and a small proposed clinic or birth-educator QR pilot. Show a relevant existing page before asking someone to install. | Prepare a reviewable pilot brief and destination list first. Outreach, agreements, publishing, paid placements and acquisition spend need their own authorization. Do not assume the Swedish distribution mix works in Paraguay. |
| 6 | Preserve the established free-core policy while investigating willingness to pay for optional additional value. A competitor's premium tier does not justify a new paywall. The current policy is recorded in [the monetization plan, section 3](archive/MVP-AND-MONETISATION.md). | Conduct owner-approved interviews about a concrete optional proposition before billing implementation. No payment provider, price, checkout or premium entitlement changes follow from this report. Current platform billing requirements would need fresh verification before any implementation. |
| 7 | Consider contextual partnerships only after validating a local user need and an actual partner service. Start with a reviewable editorial/referral proposal rather than importing an insurance model or invented directory entries. | Document user benefit, commercial disclosure, independence of clinical content, data boundaries and owner approval. No external contact or commercial contract has been initiated. |

Do not assume that expensive 3D, a large feature count, generic courses, a community, internationalization or more reminder frequency would produce growth. Each would need its own concrete problem, cost and validation. Original editorial images may improve comprehension and presentation; their effect on retention remains a hypothesis.

## Decisions and further consent

This report changes documentation only. It does not authorize any of the following:

- New analytics, pixels, attribution SDKs, advertising identifiers, tracking or changes to existing data collection/retention.
- Broader family visibility, changed sharing defaults, automatic invitations or external messages.
- A subscription, paywall, payment integration, paid acquisition, purchase, new service or additional generation spend beyond the owner's separately approved image budget.
- Clinic/partner outreach, directory publication, commercial referrals, lead sharing, sponsorship terms or insurance offerings.
- New clinical body copy, food verdict publication, altered measurements, diagnosis/personalized treatment, or bypassing the existing review gates.
- Changes to Google login, account requirements, health consent, sync, stored records, installation, service-worker/offline contracts or notification preferences.

Existing authorized design and imagery work remains governed by its own scope and budget. An attractive benchmark or this research document is not blanket implementation approval. No Preggers/Flo affiliation should be implied.
