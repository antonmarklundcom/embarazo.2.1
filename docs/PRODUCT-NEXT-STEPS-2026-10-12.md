# Next 20 app improvements — 12 October 2026

These are suggestions for the next product round, not features all implemented tonight. Existing versions of some features can be improved rather than duplicated.

1. **Clinical review first.** Have a Paraguay-based obstetric professional review exact priority guides and emergency wording, with named approvals and dates.

2. **A shorter pregnancy onboarding.** Make pregnant-mother setup the main path; move planning and companion choices to secondary entrances and reduce repeated confirmations.

3. **Three useful home actions.** Offer three actions for her current stage and let her pin favorites, instead of presenting the whole toolbox at equal priority.

4. **A clear offline download screen.** Let her download chosen weeks/guides over Wi-Fi, see their size and know what remains available without connection.

5. **A compassionate pause/archive flow.** Support loss, a paused pregnancy and a new pregnancy without unwanted reminders or family updates; first add a proper pregnancy identity model.

6. **Verified local care options.** Replace empty directory promises with real USF, IPS and private-care contacts, dated confirmation and usable opening/appointment instructions.

7. **Native Guaraní review.** Review the existing jopara with native speakers before activation; prioritize navigation and help where translation actually improves understanding.

8. **Visit preparation by appointment.** Attach the existing question list and chosen records to the next control; keep private notes excluded unless she explicitly selects them.

9. **Clinician-entered baby measurements.** Let her record measurements from an ultrasound alongside the source date, without interpreting them as a diagnosis or confusing them with fruit averages.

10. **A personal childbirth plan.** Create an editable, printable preference sheet for hospital, companion, transport and questions to discuss with her care team.

11. **A smarter hospital-bag checklist.** Separate mother/baby/documents, explain public/private differences and allow additions from her own hospital.

12. **Official care reminders.** Use clinician-reviewed Paraguay schedules and her own appointments, with quiet hours and control over notification wording.

13. **A gentle birth transition.** Lead with the mother’s recovery and practical support after birth; make the next-app/product invitation optional and well timed.

14. **Safer family permissions.** Show an exact preview of what each invitee sees; add clearer expiration and a reviewed post-birth sharing contract.

15. **More usable mood support.** Improve private mood trends and easy access to human help; avoid guilt, diagnoses or chat-based emergency decisions.

16. **Reading that fits her situation.** Let her save useful guides and choose topics for the current trimester without sending intimate diary data to a recommender.

17. **Accessible reading settings.** Test larger text, strong contrast, screen readers and one-handed controls on inexpensive phones, including a calm night-reading option.

18. **A backup restore preview.** Show which account, records and photos a backup contains and the PIN warning before replacing anything; provide a clear recovery checklist.

19. **A short real-user feedback loop.** Observe the girlfriend and other pregnant mothers completing core tasks; add an optional short feedback prompt after meaningful use.

20. **A bounded AI guide finder later.** Pilot Haiku for routing to approved guides and organizing deliberately selected questions; require opt-in, source links and spending limits. Do not add medical diagnosis chat.
